# Submission Proof Selection — 19 July 2026

## Editorial lock

This is the locked five-Insight proof set for the BIT case-study submission.
It is a curated view over the complete daily corpus, not a claim that every
stored Insight is equally strong. The full reader remains available for audit;
the final report should lead reviewers to these five items.

Selection criteria:

1. a material and specific conclusion for one of the two BIT audiences;
2. a direct causal or operational mechanism rather than category exposure;
3. primary or first-party evidence with current, inspectable chronology;
4. honest uncertainty without collapsing into an empty “could matter” claim;
5. diversity across investment transmission, adoption, evaluation, and agent
   safety rather than repeated model-routing or open-model themes.
